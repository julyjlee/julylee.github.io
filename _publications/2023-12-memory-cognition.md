This study investigates individual differences in word-frequency effects in second language (L2) visual word recognition. We compared traditional proficiency measures with lexical processing efficiency as indicators of L2 lexical quality.

**Role**: Second author — Analyzed data and proposed a new approach to measuring L2 lexical quality.
