This study examines public perceptions of cultural ecosystem services (CES) from urban green spaces (UGS) in Suwon, Korea, and Karlsruhe, Germany, through a map-based PPGIS questionnaire. The findings support theoretical perspectives such as the biophilia hypothesis, social ecology, and environmental psychology.
